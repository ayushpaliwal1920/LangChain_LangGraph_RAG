Cricket

Cricket is a popular bat-and-ball sport played between two teams of eleven players.
It is especially popular in countries such as India, Australia, England, Pakistan, New Zealand, and South Africa.

Basic Rules:
- One team bats while the other bowls and fields.
- The batting team tries to score runs.
- The bowling team tries to dismiss batters and limit runs.
- The team with the higher score wins.

Formats:
1. Test Cricket – played over up to five days.
2. One Day International (ODI) – 50 overs per side.
3. Twenty20 (T20) – 20 overs per side.

Famous Tournaments:
- ICC Cricket World Cup
- ICC T20 World Cup
- Indian Premier League (IPL)

Skills Required:
- Batting
- Bowling
- Fielding
- Strategy and teamwork

Cricket is known for its rich history, exciting matches, and passionate fan base around the world.
